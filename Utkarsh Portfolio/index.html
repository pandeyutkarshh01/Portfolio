About Me

Hi, I'm Utkarsh Pandey—a mathematics graduate, music artist, and technology enthusiast from Derhgawan, Ghazipur (Uttar Pradesh), currently based in Lucknow.
My journey has never been limited to a single path. Professionally, I work as a Travel Advisor at Kinghills Travels, where I have spent over a year helping clients plan their journeys while generating ₹39+ lakh in sales through customer-focused service and relationship building. This experience has strengthened my communication, problem-solving, and business skills.
Outside of work, music is my biggest passion. As an independent Rapper and Music Producer, I create Hip-Hop across genres including Drill, Trap, Old School, and Pop. I write, record, mix, and produce my own music, and I've released multiple tracks on major streaming platforms. Through my music, I proudly represent both my hometown, Derhgawan, and my current city, Lucknow.
Alongside my professional career and music, I'm passionate about technology and continuously learning modern software development. I enjoy building digital experiences, exploring new technologies, and creating projects that combine creativity with technical skills.
I believe growth comes from embracing different passions. Whether it's solving problems, building software, helping customers, or creating music that connects with people, I'm always driven by curiosity, creativity, and the desire to make a meaningful impact.
